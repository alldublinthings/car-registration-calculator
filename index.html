Car Registration Fee Calculator Widget

A free, self-contained widget that shows what a car costs to register in any US state. Drop it into your own site with one line of HTML.

Live preview

Pick a state and it shows the first-year registration cost and the yearly renewal. Full state-by-state breakdowns, with the source behind every fee, live on State Fee Calculator.

Add it to your site

Paste this where you want the widget to appear. It is responsive and needs no scripts of your own.

<iframe src="https://statefeecalculator.com/embed/registration-cost/" title="Car registration cost by state" width="100%" height="230" style="border:0;max-width:520px" loading="lazy"></iframe>
